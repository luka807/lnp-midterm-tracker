    How to read this. Polling average is the candidate's margin over the main opponent, averaged across polls that finished in the last 30 days (or the latest poll when none are that recent); partisan and campaign polls are included and counted. Poll shift is the change in the 21-day trailing average over the chosen window. Market odds are the candidate's latest daily price on Polymarket or Kalshi; most House district markets trade thinly, so treat them as a secondary signal. "n/a" means the forecaster does not rate the race or no figure was found.
    Sources. Ratings: Cook Political Report, Sabato's Crystal Ball and Inside Elections (via 270toWin). Markets: Polymarket, Kalshi. National averages: Silver Bulletin, FiftyPlusOne, DDHQ. Poll and cash figures are from state election pages, FEC filings and news reports; the companion spreadsheet lists them per race.
    Races marked Tracked are followed for context but are not on the Leadership Now slate, and are left out of the slate's won–lost record. New polls, market prices, ratings and results are added as dated observations, so each race keeps its full history.
